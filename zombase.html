ZomBASE

Survival · Base building · Action

You are a fugitive fleeing the zombie apocalypse. Your plane crashes on a remote, uncharted island — a place that should be deserted, yet something feels off. As the sole survivor, you must fight to stay alive, build a base from scraps, and uncover the island's dark secrets. But survival is just the beginning: the key to stopping the outbreak lies somewhere on this island. Can you find it before the zombies — and the island itself — consume you?

With an infinite defense mode that pushes your limits and a SandBox mode that gives you creative freedom, ZomBASE delivers a survival experience that keeps you coming back.
